Think refined resort formal: elegant silhouettes, breathable fabrics, rich color, and no need to match the wedding party exactly.

For the ladies

Long or midi dresses, elegant jumpsuits, or refined Filipiniana-inspired details are welcome. Choose one of the palette families or a complementary neutral. Please avoid bridal white, ivory, and extremely bright neon tones.

For the gentlemen

Barong Tagalog, a lightweight suit, or tailored separates work beautifully. Midnight navy, forest, charcoal, and warm neutrals are especially suited to the setting. Formal leather shoes or polished loafers are ideal.
